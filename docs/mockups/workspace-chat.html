What liability caps did we agree with Acme, and where?

We agreed a 12-month-fees cap with carve-outs for IP infringement. The cap appears in the Acme MSA (Section 9.2, Limitation of Liability) and is referenced by the Q3 Statement of Work. Direct damages for either party are limited to the fees paid in the preceding twelve months; the carve-outs exclude IP infringement indemnities and breaches of confidentiality from the cap.
